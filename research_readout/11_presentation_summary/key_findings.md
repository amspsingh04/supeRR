# Key findings

Ordered by importance for the presentation. Every number links to a file in this pack.

1. **'High pressure' is operationally the six balls after a wicket (batting) or after a boundary (bowling).** For batters the figure is 99.94% of high-band balls; for bowlers, 99.98% follow a four or six in the previous six. Occasion, phase, wickets and required rate together explain only 7.1% of batter pressure variance. *(06_shock/shock_event_summary.csv, 02_pressure/pressure_summary.csv)*
2. **A ball-19 indexing bug affects every pressure value through the scale.** Run of play on ball 19 is pinned near 1, and the bowler run-of-play scale is inflated (1.107 against 0.593). With the bug removed (a diagnostic, not official), the bowler ranking moves: Spearman 0.958, top-20 overlap 14/20. Batters barely move (0.998, 20/20). *(09_limitations BUG-01, 08_validation/ablation_summary.csv)*
3. **The pressure layer changes the ranking a lot, the exact weights much less.** Baseline-only against the full model shares 7/20 of the batting top 20 and 11/20 of the bowling top 20. Equal weights still give Spearman 0.980 for batters and 0.983 for bowlers. *(08_validation/ablation_summary.csv)*
4. **The top of each table.** Batters: 1. Rilee Rossouw, 2. Tim David, 3. Mahendra Singh Dhoni, 4. George Linde, 5. Ashutosh Sharma. Bowlers: 1. Corbin Bosch, 2. Matthew Humphreys, 3. Haider Ali, 4. Mujeeb Ur Rahman, 5. Imad Wasim. *(07_rankings)*
5. **Small samples sit at the top of the bowling table.** 4 of the top-10 bowlers have a high-band n_eff below 60. The median high-band n_eff is 85.7 for the top 20 against 92.8 for all ranked bowlers. *(07_rankings/final_bowler_rankings.csv)*
6. **The role split creates the batting role mix.** The top 20 is [6, 7, 7] (top/middle/lower) with role-split z-scores and [13, 6, 1] pooled. Your 12/5/3 reproduces only in the legacy 8-term model ([12, 5, 3]). *(08_validation/role_ranking_representation.csv)*
7. **Expectation models are well calibrated on average but barely discriminate.** xR: slope 0.985, R² 0.0133, MAE 1.304 against 1.328 for a constant. xW: AUC 0.559. *(05_expectation_models/expectation_model_metrics.csv)*
8. **The missing wickets-fallen feature biases ball value.** At 8–9 down, batters average -0.421 per ball and their bowlers 0.391. *(05_expectation_models/residual_by_wickets_fallen.csv)*
9. **Elo is stable and well inside its bounds.** Steps were not halved. Batters range 931–2572, bowlers 1060–2108. Half or double steps leave the composite at Spearman ≥ 0.985. *(04_elo)*
10. **Form has been removed (seven terms, consistency 0.10).** The old 8-term ranking differs only slightly: Spearman 0.989 for batters and 0.991 for bowlers. Its look-ahead (KI-01) no longer applies. *(08_validation/ablation_summary.csv)*
11. **The 2025 final's wicket timeline is verified.** PBKS lost wickets at legal balls 30, 51, 58, 73, 98, 100, 104, matching the brief. As delivery numbers, including wides and no-balls, they are 31, 52, 59, 75, 101, 103, 107. *(06_shock/shock_innings_timeline.csv)*
