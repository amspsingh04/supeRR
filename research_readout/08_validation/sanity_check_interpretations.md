## Interpretation (analyst reading of the numbers above)

- **The established elite batters sit in the 30s and 40s, not the top 10.** Kohli is 30, Buttler 34, Miller 44. On baseline alone they would be 14, 16 and 22. Their high-band and clutch terms are modest, so the pressure layer pulls them down relative to finishers. Whether that is right depends on accepting that 'pressure' means 'after a wicket' (M-01).
- **Sooryavanshi is the baseline leader (1st), but the full composite holds him at 7.** His clutch (-2.17) and consistency (-3.00, at the cap) offset a near-maximal baseline (+2.46) and peers (+3.00). His high-band sample is 41.6 weighted balls, close to the floor of 30.
- **Gurbaz rises from 182 on baseline to 40.** Almost all of the rise is clutch (+3.00, at the cap) and the high band (+1.34). It is the largest single example of the pressure layer overriding raw output, and it rests on a high-band n_eff of 68.0.
- **The two top bowlers are small-sample, pressure-driven ranks.** Bosch (1st, 367 balls, clutch +3.00, high band +2.64) and Humphreys (2nd, 305 balls, high n_eff 31.3) both lead on pressure terms. Bumrah is 7th on a much larger sample (1708 balls), with the top IPL bowler Elo (2108). Read the top two with caution (M-06).
- **Rashid Khan ranks 16th as a batter and 118th as a bowler.** His bowling clutch is -2.67 and his high band -0.80, yet his baseline-only bowling rank is 34 and his T20I Elo is 2050. BUG-03 can only raise a bowler's clutch, so the negative value is not caused by it. As executed, his high-band spells, which are overs bowled just after a boundary (M-01), cost more on average than his career rate. A spell below his own rate counts in full, whatever his team-mates did. This is the most counter-intuitive result in the sanity set.
- **Dhoni (3rd) and Tim David (2nd) fit the finisher pattern.** Both score heavily on medium, high and clutch terms. Dhoni's consistency is -2.05; David's peers term is +3.00 (at the cap).
- **Imad Wasim's batting misses the floor by 1.1 weighted balls** (n_eff 118.9). His bowling ranks 5th.
